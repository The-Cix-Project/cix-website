# ADR 0003: Keep product authority in Cix repositories

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-02

## Decision

Claims about Cix behavior must be checked against the Cix source repositories, their mission, architecture, guides, ADRs, code, OpenAPI contract, and Cix Cache. This site may summarize them and show dated proof, but it must not invent a second specification.

## Consequences

Pages link to source, API, guides, and cache evidence. Reproduced workflows record revision and capture context. If a capability is uncertain or staging-only, the page says so instead of turning intent into a product claim.
