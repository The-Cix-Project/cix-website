Known boundaries

Not hidden in the small print.

Examples of limits that matter when evaluating Cix today. This is not exhaustive; the current roadmap is the source of truth.

  • Multi-host coordinationIntentionally not designed yet. Current architecture must not foreclose it, but it makes no distributed-systems promise.
  • Hardware verificationSome positive device paths—particularly real GPU and physical-NIC scenarios—require hardware unavailable to the development sandbox.
  • Container isolation boundaryThese are namespace containers, not virtual machines. Device assignment does not provide a VFIO/IOMMU hypervisor boundary.
  • Rolling does not mean newestRolling describes the delivery model. It does not claim every component always tracks its latest upstream release.
